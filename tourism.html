Discover Kauswagan

Explore the vibrant attractions, peaceful retreats, and historic spots in our barangay.

Rivers & Scenic Spots

Cagayan de Oro River (Western Bank)

Bordering Kauswagan, the majestic Cagayan de Oro river offers stunning sunset views and a cool breeze. It serves as a beautiful natural boundary for our barangay.

📍 Puntod-Kauswagan Bridge

Kauswagan Riverwalk

A scenic stretch along the riverbank perfect for early morning jogs, leisurely evening strolls, and enjoying the peaceful atmosphere away from the city traffic.

📍 Kauswagan Boulevard

Churches & Places of Worship

San Isidro Labrador Parish (Eco Church)

The spiritual heart of Kauswagan. This beautiful parish is a gathering place for the faithful and features stunning local architecture and a peaceful courtyard.

📍 Eagle Street in Zone 1, Kauswagan

Resorts & Leisure

N Hotel & Resort

A premier destination located right in Kauswagan. Known for its expansive pool, lush gardens, and excellent dining, it is a favorite staycation spot for locals and tourists alike.

📍 Kauswagan National Highway

Historical Landmarks & Parks

Kauswagan Barangay Plaza

A well-maintained community park shaded by large trees. It serves as the venue for local fiestas, outdoor meetings, and a safe play area for children.

📍 Barangay Hall, Kauswagan

Other Notable Places

Kauswagan Commercial Hub

Experience the local hustle and bustle! This thriving strip features local delicacies, bustling street food stalls, and unique homegrown businesses.

📍 Foodhaus, Kauswagan

Your gateway to progress in Cagayan de Oro City. Dedicated to transparency, public service, and community growth.
