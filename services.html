Barangay Services

📄

Barangay Clearance

Requirements: Valid ID and Cedula (Community Tax Certificate). Issued for employment, school, or legal purposes.

🏢

Business Permit Clearance

Required for establishing or renewing a business within the barangay. Ensure all previous DTI/SEC registrations are ready.

⚖️

Lupon Tagapamayapa

Barangay justice system for filing complaints and mediating minor disputes between residents before going to court.

🩺

Barangay Health Center

Free basic check-ups, maternal care, immunizations, and medicine distribution for registered residents.

🆔

Certificate of Indigency

Issued to residents requiring financial, medical, or educational assistance from government agencies.

♻️

Solid Waste Management

Coordinate with the barangay for scheduled garbage collection, segregation guidelines, and large debris disposal.
