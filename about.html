About Barangay Kauswagan

Discover our roots, our geography, and our community.

Our History

The name "Kauswagan" is derived from the Visayan word meaning "progress" or "prosperity." True to its name, the barangay has undergone a massive transformation over the decades.

Originally, Kauswagan was primarily a vast agricultural expanse filled with rice paddies, cornfields, and coconut groves. It was a quiet, rural outskirts of the main town. However, as Cagayan de Oro City expanded westward, Kauswagan found itself in a highly strategic position. The construction of the National Highway and the bridge connecting it to the city proper across the Cagayan de Oro River accelerated its growth.

Today, the farmlands have been replaced by thriving residential subdivisions, bustling commercial centers, and vital transport terminals. From a quiet farming village, Kauswagan has successfully transitioned into one of the most dynamic, heavily populated, and progressive urban barangays in Cagayan de Oro City.

Location & Geography

Barangay Kauswagan is strategically located in the western district of Cagayan de Oro City, Misamis Oriental.

  • East: Bounded by the Cagayan de Oro River, separating it from the City Proper (Poblacion).
  • West: Bounded by Barangay Bulua.
  • North: Bounded by the coastal barangays of Bayabas and Bonbon, leading toward Macajalar Bay.
  • South: Bounded by Barangay Carmen.

Its central location makes it a major artery for transportation and commerce, acting as a gateway for travelers heading further west of the city and into the rest of Misamis Oriental.
