Local Businesses & Economy

From bustling public markets to major logistics hubs, explore the thriving economic landscape of Barangay Kauswagan.

Commercial Hubs & Markets

Town Center

Lady of Manoag Market

A bustling commercial center in Kauswagan serving as a central hub for local vendors, fresh produce, dry goods, and essential courier services for residents.

📍 Our Lady of Manaoag Town Center, Kauswagan

Retail & Dining

Kauswagan Highway Strip

The main artery of the barangay lined with fast-food chains, local carinderias, pharmacies, and hardware stores that drive the local economy.

📍 Kauswagan National Highway

Logistics & Enterprises

Logistics

Warehousing & Freight Services

Because of its strategic location near the port and coastal roads, Kauswagan is a prime spot for major delivery hubs, trucking services, and regional warehousing.

📍 Various locations in Kauswagan

Enterprise

Auto Dealerships & Service Centers

The barangay hosts numerous automotive businesses, from big-brand car dealerships to highly skilled local mechanic shops and detailing centers.
