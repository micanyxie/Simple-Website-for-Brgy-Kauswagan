About Our Barangay

True to its name—which translates to "progress" or "prosperity" in Visayan—Barangay Kauswagan is a vibrant, rapidly developing community located in the heart of Cagayan de Oro City, Misamis Oriental.

We pride ourselves on a strong community spirit, a bustling local economy, and an unwavering dedication to public service. As one of the city's key residential and commercial hubs, Kauswagan offers a welcoming, safe, and dynamic environment. Whether you are a resident, a business owner, or a visitor, you will find that in Kauswagan, community and progress grow hand in hand.
